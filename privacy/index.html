The short version

  • We collect what a dating app needs: your profile, photos, messages, and (only if you allow it) your location.
  • We do not sell your information. We have no advertisers, and the app currently contains no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Photos and chat images are checked automatically by a Google service for unsafe content before anyone else can see them.
  • You control a lot of it: you can hide your profile, hide your age, hide when you were last active, and delete your account at any time. When you delete, your data is erased straight away.
  • Your data is stored on secure cloud servers outside Bhutan, in the Asia-Pacific region. Hingtam is run from Bhutan.
  • Hingtam is for adults only (18+).

This policy explains what personal information Hingtam collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Hingtam mobile app and to hingtam.com, wherever in the world you use them.

1. Who we are

In plain EnglishHingtam is run by one person in Bhutan. Email support@hingtam.com for anything.

Hingtam is a dating app for Bhutanese people looking to make meaningful connections, and for those who want to meet them. It is run by [to be added at launch], a sole proprietor trading as Hingtam under business licence [to be added at launch], at [to be added at launch], Kingdom of Bhutan (called "Hingtam", "we" or "us" in this policy).

We decide how and why your personal information is used, which makes us the "controller" of it under laws that use that word. You can reach us at support@hingtam.com for anything in this policy.

This policy covers members in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the United States and Bhutan, and anyone else who uses Hingtam. Section 10 explains rights that depend on where you live.

2. The information we collect

In plain EnglishYour profile, photos, messages and, if you allow it, your location. We do not collect ID, face scans or your contacts.

What you give us

  • Account details — your email address, or the details Google gives us if you sign in with Google (your email address and basic profile information).
  • Your profile — your name, date of birth, gender, who you are interested in, your photos, your bio, your prompt answers and your interests.
  • Optional profile details — things you choose to add, such as height, star sign, where you were born, drinking, smoking, diet, love language, personality type and what you are looking for.
  • Your preferences — such as how far away you want to see people, and your privacy settings.
  • Your messages — the messages and images you send in conversations.
  • Reports and contact — anything you tell us when you report someone, appeal a decision, or email support.

Sensitive information

Some of what you tell us can reveal sensitive things about you: your gender and who you are interested in (which can reveal your sexual orientation), where you were born (which can suggest ethnicity or nationality), your photos, and optional lifestyle details such as drinking, smoking and diet (which can relate to health or religious practice). Giving us this information is your choice, and you can change or remove most of it at any time in Edit Profile. We use it only to run Hingtam as described in this policy: to show you to, and show you, people who may suit you. By adding it, you agree that we may use it in that way.

Your location

If you allow it, we collect your precise location from your phone. We use it only to work out how far you are from other members and to show people nearby. We store one most-recent location point, not a history of where you have been. Other members see an approximate distance in kilometres, never your coordinates. You can say no, or turn location off later in your phone settings; Hingtam still works, but distance-based matching does not.

What we record when you use Hingtam

  • Your likes, passes, Super Likes, matches, blocks, hidden profiles and reports.
  • Counts we need to apply daily limits for your plan.
  • When you were last active. Other members may see this (for example "Active now") unless you turn it off in Settings.
  • Your subscription plan (Free, Pro or Premium) and whether it is billed monthly or yearly. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details.
  • Basic sign-in and session data that keeps you securely logged in, and technical records (such as your IP address and the time of a request) that our service providers keep for security and fixing faults.

What we do not collect

  • No government ID documents, face scans or other biometric data.
  • No access to your phone contacts, microphone or advertising ID.
  • No history of your movements.

If we ever add something like ID or selfie verification, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

3. How we use your information, and why

In plain EnglishTo run the app, keep people safe, and email you about your account. Never for ads.

Privacy laws in some countries require us to say which legal reason we rely on. Here they are, next to each use:

  • To run Hingtam — create your profile, show you candidates, match you, deliver messages, apply your plan's features, and keep your account working. Reason: it is necessary to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • To work out distance — using your location, if you allow it. Reason: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To use sensitive information you choose to add — as described in section 2. Reason: your explicit consent.
  • To keep people safe — screening photos and images, reviewing reports, banning accounts that break our rules, stopping banned people from returning, and preventing scams and abuse. Reason: our legitimate interest in a safe service, and legal obligations.
  • To contact you about your account — one-time sign-in codes, email-change confirmations and important notices. We do not currently send marketing emails; if we ever do, we will ask first. Reason: contract.
  • To improve and secure the app — finding and fixing faults and spotting misuse, using the information described above. Reason: our legitimate interest.
  • To meet legal duties and handle disputes — including responding to lawful requests and protecting our rights. Reason: legal obligation and legitimate interest.

Automated decisions

  • Safety screening. Every photo and chat image is checked by an automated service (Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch) for nudity and graphic violence before anyone else can see it. If an image is rejected, it is not saved. Automated checks can make mistakes; if you think a photo was wrongly rejected, email support@hingtam.com and a person will look at it.
  • Who you see, and in what order. Candidates are chosen using your preferences (who you are interested in, distance, and blocks) and ordered using a simple score based on whether you want the same thing, how many interests you share, and whether they were recently active. "Top Picks" are ordered by recent likes. This ordering never hides someone who matches your preferences; it only changes the order. It does not use your sensitive information to exclude anyone.

4. Who can see your information

In plain EnglishOther members see what you choose to show. You can hide your age, your active status, or your whole profile.
  • Other members can see the profile information you choose to show: first name, photos, bio, prompts, interests, the optional details you add, an approximate distance, and (unless you turn it off) your age and when you were last active. Members you match with can also see your messages.
  • Paying members have extra tools, such as seeing who has liked them. They see only what other members see.
  • Screenshots. We cannot stop another member copying or screenshotting what you share. Only share what you are comfortable with.

You control visibility: Hide my profile removes you from other people's decks, Show my age and Show when I'm active can be switched off, and you can block or hide anyone.

5. Who we share it with

In plain EnglishOnly the companies that help run the app, and the police when the law requires it. We never sell your data.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run Hingtam, who may use your information only to provide their service to us: Supabase (our database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in the Asia-Pacific region); Google (Google Cloud Vision for photo and image safety checks, and Google sign-in if you choose it); Resend (account emails such as sign-in codes); and Apple and Google (app store billing and, in future, notifications).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or when we believe it is necessary to protect someone's life or safety, to report child sexual exploitation or other serious crime, or to protect our rights. Where we are allowed to, we will tell you first.
  • A new owner, if Hingtam is ever sold or merged, on terms that keep this policy's protections for your information.
  • Anyone else, only with your clear permission.

6. Where your information is stored and sent

In plain EnglishOn secure cloud servers outside Bhutan. We run Hingtam from Bhutan.

Hingtam is operated from Bhutan. Your information is stored on secure cloud servers in the Asia-Pacific region, outside Bhutan, and we access it from Bhutan. Our service providers may handle it in other countries too, including the United States (for example, Google's image-safety check). Bhutan does not have a general data protection law of the kind some countries have, so we commit to applying this policy to everyone, wherever they live. Where a law requires safeguards for sending information abroad, we use them (such as written agreements with our providers).

7. How long we keep your information

In plain EnglishWhile you have an account. Delete it and your data goes straight away.
  • While you have an account — we keep what you give us so Hingtam works.
  • When you delete your account (Settings, then Delete Account) — we erase your profile, photos, interests, prompt answers, likes, matches, blocks, reports, and your messages (including images you sent) straight away. This also removes the conversations from the other person's side. Nothing is kept for later "just in case".
  • The one exception: banned accounts. If an account was banned, we keep a scrambled, one-way fingerprint of its email address (not the address itself, and nothing else about the person) for as long as needed to stop that email being used to sign up again.
  • Photos that fail safety screening are not saved.
  • Backups and logs. Our hosting provider may keep short-term backups and technical logs. Deleted information drops out of backups as they rotate, normally within 30 days, and we do not restore deleted accounts.
  • Legal reasons. If we are legally required to preserve something, or need it to handle a legal claim or an investigation, we keep only what is needed for as long as it is needed.

8. Your choices and controls

In plain EnglishEdit, hide, block, report or delete, all inside the app.
  • Edit or remove your profile details, photos, prompts and interests in Edit Profile.
  • Privacy settings in Settings: hide your profile, hide your age, and hide when you were last active.
  • Location: change it in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Block, hide or report anyone. Reporting someone also blocks them.
  • Delete your account in Settings. If you cannot get into the app, email support@hingtam.com from the address on your account and we will do it for you.

9. Your rights

In plain EnglishYou can ask to see, fix or delete your data. We reply within 30 days.

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information (you can also just delete your account);
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to a use you do not agree with;
  • give you your information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw a consent you gave us (for example, for location).

To make a request, email support@hingtam.com from the email address on your account (so we know it is you) or use the in-app controls. We aim to reply within 30 days. We will not charge a fee unless a request is clearly excessive. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to us first, and you always have the right to complain to your local regulator.

10. Rights that depend on where you live

In plain EnglishSpecial rules in Australia, Canada, New Zealand and the US apply, and we follow them.
  • Australia — we handle your information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988. You can ask for access to, or correction of, your information, and complain to us or to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
  • Canada — we follow the principles of PIPEDA: we collect only what we tell you about, use it only for those purposes unless we ask you again, and you can access and correct it. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).
  • New Zealand — under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask for access to, and correction of, your information. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz). If a privacy breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Commissioner.
  • United States — residents of California and other states with privacy laws can ask us to tell them what we collect, delete it, or correct it. In the last 12 months we have collected identifiers (such as email and name), profile and photo content, messages, precise location (if allowed), sensitive details you choose to add (such as sexual orientation and ethnicity-related information), and usage records, for the purposes in section 3. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for advertising across other companies' sites or apps, and we use sensitive information only to provide the service, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not treat you worse for using your rights. You can use an authorized agent, and if we refuse a request you can appeal by replying to our answer. We respond within 45 days.
  • Bhutan — Bhutan's privacy rules are mainly in the Information, Communications and Media Act 2018 (online privacy and unlawful disclosure of data) and the National Digital Identity Act 2023. We apply this policy's rights to every member in Bhutan, and you can also raise concerns with the relevant Bhutanese authority.
  • Everywhere else — you have the same rights described in section 9.

11. Children

In plain EnglishAdults only. We remove anyone under 18.

Hingtam is for people who are 18 or older. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and do not let anyone under 18 create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 is using Hingtam, tell us at support@hingtam.com or use Report in the app. We will remove the account and its information. Any content involving minors is banned and may be reported to the authorities.

12. How we protect your information

In plain EnglishEncrypted on the way, private photo storage, and very limited access.
  • Information is encrypted while it travels between your phone and our servers.
  • Our hosting provider encrypts stored data, and photos are kept in private storage that is shared with other members only through short-lived links.
  • Access rules in the database mean a member can only read what they are allowed to see.
  • Only a very small number of people can reach the systems, and administrator actions need two-step verification.
  • Sign-in uses one-time email codes or Google sign-in, so there are no passwords for us to lose.

No system is completely safe. If a breach is likely to harm you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires, without undue delay, and explain what happened and what you can do.

13. Our website

In plain EnglishNo cookies, no tracking, and no fonts from other companies.

hingtam.com shows information about Hingtam and our legal documents. It does not use cookies or analytics, and it does not collect anything from you. Its fonts are stored on our own site, so opening a page does not contact any other company.

14. Changes to this policy

In plain EnglishWe will tell you before big changes.

We may update this policy as Hingtam grows or the law changes. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect, and for changes that need your consent we will ask again. The "last updated" date at the top always shows the current version. If you do not agree to a change, you can delete your account.
