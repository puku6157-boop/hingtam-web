About Hingtam

A place for meaningful connections, made for the Bhutanese community.

Finding someone who understands where you come from should not be hard.

Hingtam is a dating app made for Bhutanese people looking for meaningful connections, and for everyone who loves the culture. It is for finding love, and for finding friends too.

We keep our promises simple. Be kind. Be honest. Stay safe. We never sell your information, and you can delete everything whenever you like.

Hingtam is run from Bhutan.

Kind by design

Clear rules, photo checks, and a real person who reads every report.

Private by default

No ads and no selling your data. You choose what to show, and you can hide any of it.

Made for everyone

No matter who you are and no matter where you are, everybody deserves a bit of loving.

Say hello

Questions, ideas or something to tell us? We read every message.
