  • You are responsible for your own decisions and for how you treat people, online and in person.

To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for what members say or do, on Hingtam or off it, including when you meet.

7. Reports, moderation and enforcement

In plain EnglishA person reads every report. Serious breaches mean a ban, and you can appeal.
  • Reporting. Every profile and conversation has a Report button. Reporting someone also blocks them for you straight away. A person reviews reports.
  • Automated checks. Photos and chat images are screened automatically before anyone else can see them. Automated checks are not perfect, so you can ask a person to look at a decision.
  • What we may do. If someone breaks these terms or the Community Guidelines, we may remove content, restrict features, or permanently ban the account. For serious breaches (such as scams, threats or anything involving minors) we may ban immediately, without warning.
  • Reasons and appeals. When we remove content or ban an account, we will tell you what we decided and why, unless doing so could put someone at risk or the law stops us. If you think we made a mistake, email support@hingtam.com within 90 days. A person who did not make the original decision will review it and reply, normally within 14 days.
  • Stopping return. If an account is banned, we keep a one-way scrambled fingerprint of its email address so it cannot be used to sign up again (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Serious crime. We may report illegal activity, including child exploitation, to the authorities.
